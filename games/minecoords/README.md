## Description

On a random Discord server, A dumb kid posted an image of his stash on **Doughnut SMP** 🔥. Can you find the coordinates of his stash ~~and grief his base~~?

The XZ coordinates are within (-225000, 225000).

The flag is the coordinates of the target block, e.g. `holic{123456,-30,-6767}`.
