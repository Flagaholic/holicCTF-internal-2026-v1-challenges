## Description

In holicCTF, **everyone** is an author. So if you have any brilliant ideas for a challenge, feel free to upload them!

To upload your challenge, go to our Discord BotCentre and use `/holicctf add` with botholic, then follow the steps! To change something in your uploaded chall, please open a ticket.

As we believe quality is more important than quantity, we implemented a **leaderboard** for top rated challenges / authors.

Don't feel pressured to make the best chall, after all we're playing to have fun and learn stuff together, so I'm looking forward to all of your amazing challenges on the platform!!

Please **DO NOT** attempt to upload any malicious / bad-intended challenge files. Any attempt would lead to an **IMMEDIATE BAN** from this event, our team and any future events. **You have been warned.**

Check the leaderboard channel for the flag! :>
